This policy explains how the Drone Pocket Logbook iPhone app (the "App") handles your information. The App is made by Michael Johnson, doing business as "I Like Color," an independent developer in Fort Worth, Texas ("I," "me," or "the Developer").

The short version

  • Everything stays on your iPhone. Your pilot credentials, documents, drones, and flight logs are stored only on your device, and in your own encrypted iCloud or computer backup if you turn backups on. They never go to my servers.
  • No account, no sync, no servers. I don't run any server for the App, and the App doesn't send your data to me or anyone else.
  • I never receive your data. I can't see it, recover it, or hand it over to anyone, because I don't have it.
  • You are in control, and that also means you are responsible for backups and for keeping your device secure.
  • Deleting the App deletes your data stored in the App.
  • I don't sell or share personal information. No ads, no analytics, no tracking.

1. What the App stores, and where

Everything in this table is typed in or added by you, and stored only on your device.

The App doesn't need an internet connection to work. Its only network connection is to Apple's App Store (through Apple's StoreKit 2), to process and restore your Pro purchase. The App has no third-party SDKs.

2. Location

When you log a flight, the App can fill in your precise location using Apple's Core Location framework, which works on your device. It asks for "While Using the App" permission only. The App uses your location only when you log a flight, and it saves the coordinates only in your flight log on your device. The App doesn't track your location in the background, and it never sends your location to me. You can turn location off in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services. If you do, you can still type in a location by hand.

The App doesn't send your coordinates to any map, geocoding, or weather service. Weather is something you type in yourself.

3. Other permissions

iOS asks before the App can use these, and you can change them any time in Settings > Drone Pocket Logbook:

  • Face ID or Touch ID (with your device passcode as a fallback): used only to unlock the App before it shows your saved IACRA username and FTN. The App uses Apple's on-device authentication (the LocalAuthentication framework), so the check happens entirely inside iOS. The App never receives, stores, or sends your face, fingerprint, or any other biometric data; iOS only tells the App whether you were recognized. The documents vault does not have a Face ID lock. When iOS asks for permission, the App explains: "Face ID is used only to unlock your saved IACRA username and FTN."
  • Photos and Files pickers: when you add a document, you choose it with Apple's photo picker or Files picker. Neither one needs a permission prompt. The App receives only the photo or file you pick, and it can't see the rest of your photo library or files.
  • Notifications: used only for the expiry reminders you set. These are local notifications created on your device. They are not push notifications from a server, and no server is involved.

4. Quick-Reference Card

The Quick-Reference Card shows the credential details you entered full-screen, so you can refer to them quickly, for example if an official asks. It is a reference, not an official document.

5. Exports and sharing

Pro lets you export your flight log as a PDF or CSV. An export only leaves your device if you send it somewhere yourself (for example, by email, AirDrop, or saving to Files or iCloud Drive). After that, the app or service you choose handles it. Exports never include your IACRA username or FTN. Those two items live only in the Keychain. Treat exported files as sensitive: they can contain certificate numbers, serial numbers, and flight locations.

6. Backups, device security, and data loss: your responsibility

  • No sync. The App does not use iCloud sync or any other sync service.
  • Device backups. Your profile, hangar, and flight log are included in your own encrypted iCloud Backup or computer backup, if you turn backups on. That is Apple's standard iOS behavior. These backups belong to you and are handled by Apple. They never go to my servers, and I can't access them. Documents vault files are excluded from backups, and your IACRA username and FTN are stored in the Keychain as "this device only," so they are never restored to another device. If your phone is lost or broken, your vault documents and IACRA record are gone unless you still have the originals.
  • You are responsible for backups. I can't restore lost data, because I never have it. Export your log regularly and keep the original documents.
  • You are responsible for device security. Use a passcode and Face ID or Touch ID, keep iOS updated, and don't share your unlocked phone. Anyone who can unlock your phone may be able to see your data (except your IACRA username and FTN, which need an extra Face ID, Touch ID, or passcode check). The documents vault has no separate lock, so anyone who can unlock your phone and open the App can see your documents.

7. Deleting your data

  • Delete items one at a time: inside the App, you can delete individual flights, drones, maintenance entries, documents, and your saved IACRA login (username and FTN). The App doesn't have a single "erase all" button.
  • Delete everything: delete the App from your iPhone. Deleting the App deletes everything stored in it. iOS can keep Keychain items after an app is deleted, so the App cleans out its Keychain items the first time it opens after a reinstall. Your old IACRA username and FTN can't come back.
  • Copies you exported yourself, or older device backups, are not deleted. Delete those separately.

8. Purchases

The App is a free download. Pro is an optional one-time in-app purchase of $7.99 USD. Apple handles payment through the App Store using StoreKit 2, and I don't receive your name, Apple Account, or payment details. Apple tells the App only whether Pro is unlocked. Pro is not shared through Apple's Family Sharing. See Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

9. Analytics, crash reports, ads, and tracking

The App has no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs (no third-party SDKs at all), and it does not track you across other apps or websites. If you choose to share analytics with app developers in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements, Apple may give me anonymous, aggregated crash and usage statistics. These do not identify you.

10. Selling and sharing

I do not sell, rent, or share personal information, and I don't use it for targeted advertising. I don't receive it in the first place. I can't give your data to the FAA, law enforcement, insurers, or anyone else, because I don't have it.

11. Children

The App is for drone pilots and is not directed to children. It is not in the App Store's Kids category. The App collects no personal information from anyone, so it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent me information (for example, by email), contact me and I will delete it.

12. Your privacy rights (Texas, California, other U.S. states, EU/UK)

Laws such as the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, the California Consumer Privacy Act, and the EU and UK GDPR give people rights to access, correct, delete, or get a copy of their personal data, and to opt out of its sale, targeted advertising, or profiling. Some of the data you store, such as precise location and government-issued ID numbers, is considered sensitive. But it is processed only on your device, under your control, and I don't collect or hold it. You can view, correct, export, and delete all of it yourself in the App. I don't sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

If you email me for support, I'll use your email address and message only to reply, then delete them on request. For EU/UK users, my legal basis for handling a support email is my legitimate interest in answering you. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. For any privacy request, or to appeal my response to a request, email steadyprompterapp@gmail.com. I'll respond within 45 days.

13. Changes to this policy

If I ever change how the App handles data (for example, by adding iCloud sync), I'll update this policy and the App Store privacy label before the change ships, and I'll tell you in the App or the release notes. A feature that sends data off your device would be optional.
