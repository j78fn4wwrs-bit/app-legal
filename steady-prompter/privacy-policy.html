This policy explains how the Steady Prompter iPhone app (the "App") handles your information. The App is made by Michael Johnson, doing business as "I Like Color," an independent developer in Fort Worth, Texas ("I," "me," or "the Developer").

The short version

  • I don't collect your data. The App has no account, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no servers of my own.
  • Your scripts stay on your iPhone. I never see them.
  • The camera and microphone are used only when you record. Your videos are saved to your own Photos library, not to me.
  • I don't sell or share your personal information with anyone, for any reason.
  • Purchases are handled by Apple. I never see your payment details.

1. Information the App stores on your device

The App keeps the following only on your device, in the App's private storage:

What Where it lives Do I receive it?
Your scripts (title, text, and when you created or edited them) On your device, in the App's storage No
Display settings (scroll speed, font size, mirror, colors, and similar) On your device, in the App's settings No
Text files you choose to import Copied into the App on your device. The App reads only the file you pick. No
Recordings (Pro feature) Saved to your Photos library. While recording, the video is held briefly in the App's temporary folder. It is deleted after it is saved, or if saving fails. No

I don't operate any server that receives this information. The App doesn't send your scripts, settings, or recordings anywhere.

2. Camera, microphone, and Photos permissions

iOS asks for your permission before the App can use these. You can say no, and you can change your mind at any time in Settings > Steady Prompter.

  • Camera: used only when you open the recording screen (a Pro feature), so you can see yourself and record while your script scrolls. If you say no, the teleprompter still works; you just can't record.
  • Microphone: used only while recording, so your video has sound. If you say no, your recordings will be silent.
  • Photos (add only): used only to save finished recordings to your Photos library. The App asks for "add only" access, so it cannot see or read your existing photos and videos.

The App doesn't use the camera or microphone in the background, and it doesn't analyze, upload, or keep copies of your face, voice, or video. The App doesn't use speech recognition. While recording, the video is held briefly in the App's temporary folder. It is deleted after it is saved to Photos, and it is also deleted if saving fails.

3. Purchases

The App is free to download. "Pro" is an optional one-time in-app purchase of $9.99 USD, with no subscription. Apple processes the purchase through the App Store. Apple tells the App only whether Pro is unlocked, so it can turn on Pro features and let you restore your purchase. Pro is not shared through Apple's Family Sharing. I don't receive your name, Apple Account, or payment information. Apple's handling of your purchase is covered by Apple's privacy policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/.

4. iCloud, backups, and sync

  • The App does not use iCloud sync, CloudKit, or any other sync service.
  • If you have iCloud Backup or computer (Finder) backups turned on, iOS may include the App's data (your scripts and settings) in your device backup. Those backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by me. I can't access them.
  • Recordings saved to Photos follow your Photos settings. For example, if you use iCloud Photos, Apple will sync them.

5. Analytics, crash reports, and advertising

  • The App contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking code, and no third-party SDKs.
  • The App's only network connection is to Apple's App Store (StoreKit), to process and restore your Pro purchase.
  • The App does not track you across other companies' apps or websites, and it never asks for permission to do so.
  • Apple crash and usage data: If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone settings (Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give me anonymous, aggregated crash reports and usage statistics through App Store Connect. These do not identify you. You can turn this off at any time in that same menu.

6. Sharing and selling

I do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information, and I don't use it for targeted advertising. I don't have your personal information to begin with. When you share a script or video yourself (for example, through the iOS share sheet or from Photos), the app or person you share it with handles it under their own terms.

7. Deleting your data

  • Delete a script: swipe to delete it in the App.
  • Delete everything: deleting the App from your iPhone deletes the scripts and settings stored in it. (They may still be in older device backups you made.)
  • Recordings: these are in your Photos library. Delete them in the Photos app. Deleting the App does not delete videos you saved to Photos.

8. Children

The App is a general-audience productivity tool. It is not made for or marketed to children, and it is not in the App Store's Kids category. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 where that age applies). If you believe a child has sent me information (for example, by emailing me), contact me and I will delete it.

9. Your privacy rights (U.S. states, Texas, California, EU/UK)

Privacy laws such as the Texas Data Privacy and Security Act, the California Consumer Privacy Act, and the EU and UK GDPR give people rights to access, correct, delete, or copy their personal data, and to opt out of its sale or use for targeted advertising. The App doesn't collect or process your personal data on any server, so in practice I hold nothing to access, correct, delete, or export. Everything is on your device and under your control. I never sell personal data or use it for targeted advertising.

If you contact me (for example, by email for support), I will use your email address and message only to reply. I will keep them only as long as needed to help you, then delete them on request. For EU/UK users, my legal basis for handling a support email is my legitimate interest in answering you. You may also complain to your local data protection authority. You can make any privacy request, or appeal a decision I make about your request, by emailing steadyprompterapp@gmail.com. I will respond within 45 days.

10. Security

Your data is protected by your iPhone's own security, such as your passcode, Face ID, and iOS data protection. Keep your device locked and up to date. Because I don't store your data, a breach of my systems can't expose it.

11. Changes to this policy

If I change how the App handles data (for example, by adding iCloud sync or analytics), I will update this policy and the "Last updated" date before the change ships. If the change is significant, I'll also tell you in the App or in the App Store release notes. Any new data collection would be optional where the law requires it.
